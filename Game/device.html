        How to play

        Batting: drag to aim; tap Swing just before the ball arrives.

        Pitching: choose a pitch, aim, then tap Pitch.

        Defense: move the selected fielder toward the ball target. Use Slide or Dive to reach; tap a bright base marker after securing the ball. Teammates cover the other bases.

        Prototype: unfinished character views use named markers. Extra-base running, tags and tag-ups remain in development.
